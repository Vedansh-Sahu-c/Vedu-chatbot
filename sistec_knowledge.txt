--- SECTION: OVERVIEW ---
SISTec Gandhi Nagar Campus is a premier private engineering institution located in Gandhi Nagar, Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh, India. It operates under the aegis of the Sagar Group of Institutions. The campus is situated opposite Raja Bhoj International Airport, Bhopal (M.P.) 462036.

--- SECTION: PLACEMENTS AND TRAINING ---
SISTec has a dedicated Training and Placement Cell (T and P Cell) that prepares students for campus placements. Training modules are called Kaizen. Kaizen includes: Mock Interviews, Technical Workshops, Communication and Soft Skills Training, Aptitude and Reasoning Sessions, Resume Building. SISTec has signed MoUs with Smartbridge Educational Services and Cybrom Technology for practical industry-aligned learning.

--- SECTION: SWIMMING POOL ---
SISTec is the ONLY private engineering college in Bhopal with a six-lane state-of-the-art swimming pool. The pool has all modern amenities. Swimming instructors are available round the clock. The college has hosted several state-level swimming events.

--- SECTION: SPORTS GROUNDS ---
The college has separate cricket and football grounds along with volleyball courts. Students participate in inter-institution, inter-district, and inter-division tournaments. Students represent RGPV in All India Inter-University tournaments annually. The college hosts in-house competitions and tournaments.

--- SECTION: GYMNASIUM ---
An on-site gym at SISTec is accessible round the clock. It is well-equipped to help students relieve stress and maintain fitness. More affordable than a regular gym membership.

--- SECTION: HOSTEL ---
SISTec provides separate hostel facilities for boys and girls. Hostel rooms have beds, mattresses, study tables, wardrobes, 24-hour water supply, and Wi-Fi. Resident students have evening access to library, computer labs, and sports grounds.

--- SECTION: MESS ---
The mess serves four meals daily to hostel students with calorific and nutritive balance. Can feed around 500 students at one time. Vegetarian food is served. Mess charges collected annually. Parents, guests, and day scholars can use the mess at minimal charges.

--- SECTION: CANTEEN ---
Spacious canteen surrounded by trees with outdoor seating. Serves south Indian food, north Indian delicacies, snacks, main course, and desserts. Diversified menu to suit all tastes.

--- SECTION: LIBRARY ---
Large collection of engineering and management books, e-journals, and databases. Integrated with Mastersoft LIBMAN and MOPAC mobile app. Member of DELNET, National Digital Library of India (NDL), and RGPV Consortium e-Library. Reading room capacity: 150 students. Uses barcode system and mobile app for transactions. Uninterrupted internet access for e-resources.

--- SECTION: WIFI CAMPUS ---
Entire campus including all buildings and hostels is covered with Wi-Fi 24 hours a day 7 days a week. Internet uses a 500 Mbps leased line from FiberNet. Network is safe-search filtered with security protocols. AV Rooms, Conference Halls, Auditoriums, Computer Labs equipped with high-speed internet. Users get secure login ID and password for Wi-Fi access.

--- SECTION: TRANSPORT ---
SISTec has a fleet of over 100 buses providing transport to students and staff across Bhopal. Buses cover all important routes including remote localities.

--- SECTION: CONFERENCE AND AV HALLS ---
State-of-the-art conference and meeting rooms for academic events and corporate recruitment. Every department has its own Audio-Visual Hall (AV Hall) with LCD projectors, screens, and multimedia facilities for presentations and expert lectures.

--- SECTION: ATM ---
